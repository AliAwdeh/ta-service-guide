# Design brief — TA Service Guide (maids.cc)

- **Design read**: UAE families who have just hired a maid through maids.cc Travel Assist and want to know what happens next, stage by stage. They open this from a WhatsApp message on their phone, often on mobile data. Register: reassuring, official, effortless.
- **Concept spine**: "the road from there to your doorstep" — one continuous numbered path from the first document to airport pickup, so the client can always see which stage they are in and what is left.
- **Delivery tier**: editorial — calm, typographic, mobile-first; micro-motion only (scroll-spy tabs, smooth section jumps).
- **Locked palette** (inherited from [maid-visa-guide](https://github.com/AliAwdeh/maid-visa-guide), which is itself the client's existing brand deck — override of catalog bans): navy `#111827` text, brand blue `#4878BC`, soft blue wash `#EEF3FB`, accent orange `#F6891E` (secondary callouts + alternating stage markers), confirmation green `#15B886` / `#0A7C5A` (the guarantee and parallel-track notes), borders `#B9CCE6` / `#E5E7EB`. Defense: a client frequently receives both this guide and the visa guide; continuity between two official documents from the same company beats novelty in either.
- **Locked type**: system humanist sans stack (`ui-sans-serif`/system-ui) — the page must load instantly inside WhatsApp's in-app browser; no webfont blocking.
- **Tier-1 technique**: sticky segmented section nav with scroll-spy over a vertical numbered stepper. Enacts the spine: the reader always knows where they are on the journey.
- **Section plan**: hero (logo + "Your {Role} Service Guide" + which guide this is) → centered italic preamble → 1. The Process (numbered stepper with document/info/breakdown callouts) → 2. Your Timeline at a Glance → 3. Our Guarantee to You → closing note. One layout family per section; three sections so every nav tab fits on a phone with no horizontal scroll.
- **Asset plan**: inline SVG icon set (stroke, brand blue/orange), 13 glyphs mapped by name from the content data; maids.cc logo + favicon served locally. No external requests — nothing to block on a slow connection.
- **CTA inventory**: none — informational document; the only interactive chrome is section navigation.
- **Typography note**: body copy is justified from `sm` up only. The source document is justified throughout, but these paragraphs are long and a phone column is ~330px wide, where justification opens visible rivers between words. Left-aligned on phones, justified on tablet and desktop.
- **Content note**: all copy is data in `src/lib/guide-content.ts`, with `{role}`/`{subj}`/`{poss}` placeholders resolved per client — so one set of strings serves a female maid and a male driver, and no component knows anything about the Philippines or Nepal.
- **Variant note**: 14 guides share this one layout. Stage count ranges from 2 (Ugandan, Visa Only) to 6 (Filipina), and the Filipina guide alone adds titled sub-sections and an emirate-dependent fee comparison — the stepper absorbs both without a second layout family.
